# Sideveil privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Sideveil is a browser extension that requests or activates the native temporary/incognito mode for new ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations.

## Information used on your device

Sideveil stores three on/off preferences in your browser’s local extension storage. These preferences control automation for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It does not use browser sync storage.

On supported sites, Sideveil inspects the current page URL and relevant interface controls, such as the temporary-chat button and mode indicator. When you open the popup, it identifies the current supported service and requests its temporary-mode status from the local content script. This processing happens in your browser.

## Information not collected by Sideveil

Sideveil has no analytics, developer backend, or account system. It does not collect or log your prompts, model responses, credentials, contacts, payment details, or browsing history. It does not send your chat data or local preferences to the developer or to third-party analytics services. It does not sell data or use it for advertising.

All extension code and fonts are included in the package. The extension does not load remote code or request external fonts.

## AI services and extension stores

Your messages are still sent to the AI service you choose. Sideveil does not replace that service’s privacy policy, retention rules, account requirements, or temporary-chat limitations. The service may retain temporary chats under its own policies. The browser extension stores may process installation, account, or review information under their own policies.

Sideveil does not provide anonymity, network encryption, a VPN, browser Incognito/InPrivate windows, or retroactive deletion of saved chats. It cannot verify a provider’s internal data handling.

## Your choices

Use the popup to turn automation on or off per service. Turning a preference off stops future automation without closing the current temporary chat. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension settings under the browser’s normal uninstall behavior. To manage AI conversation data, use the relevant AI provider’s controls.

## Contact and changes

For general support or privacy questions, open an issue in the Sideveil GitHub repository from which you obtained this extension. Do not include private chats, credentials, or account details in public issues. If Sideveil is distributed through an extension store, you may also use the publisher’s support contact shown on that listing. Updates to this policy should reflect changes to the shipped extension.
